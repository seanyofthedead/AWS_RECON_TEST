## Where the numbers come from

- **Phase durations** (2–4, 4–6, 2–4 and 8–16 weeks) are our estimates, first set in the [demo review](../reviews/2026-10-05-client-demo-review-and-poc-scope.md) §12. ICE gave no timeline.
- **FTE levels per role** are our staffing judgment for a team of 3 or 4 core people. Labor hours = FTE × 40 hours × weeks, computed for the low, base and high durations. Change the FTEs and the hours change in proportion.
- **ICE staff hours** are our estimates of what we would ask for. ICE has not agreed to them.
- **Facts from ICE** (shared mailbox, technicians, proper-invoice review, FileOnQ, field approval by the COR, financial system, Treasury, vendor variety, rework) are from the Oct 5 meeting. Quotes and timestamps are in the demo review.
